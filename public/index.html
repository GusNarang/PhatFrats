PhatFrats

Your whole weekend, mapped.

PhatFrats is a party finder for Penn State students. Create an account, confirm your Penn State email with a code, and you get a live map of every Interfraternity Council house around State College, with this week's parties lit up on it. Tap a glowing house to see the theme, start time, dress code and how full it's getting, then request an invite straight from the page. Free accounts can see the whole map and request an invite to one party per week. Full Access is $0.75 per month, which removes the limit and moves your requests up the line. The full directory of all 34 chapters is open to everyone.

    Party smart

    Before you head out

    Bring your PSU ID

    Hosts check names against the invite list at the door. No list, no entry, and that's on purpose.

    Arrive with your crew, leave with your crew

    Set a meet-up spot and a time to check in. Charge your phone before you go.

    Look out for each other

    Penn State's Responsible Action Protocol is there so students call for help. If someone needs medical attention, call 911.

    Respect the house

    You're a guest in someone's home and on a residential street. Keep it down on the walk back through the Highlands.
