About PhatFrats

PhatFrats is the party map for Penn State. Every IFC house on one map, every party's theme and vibe in one place, and invite lists instead of crowds at the door. Built by a Penn State student, for Penn State students.

How it works

🎟️ For students

Sign up with your Penn State email, browse the map, and request an invite. Hosts say yes, or waitlist you. Free accounts get one request a week. Full Access is unlimited and puts you at the front of the line.

🏛️ For chapters

Chapters post their own parties, answer requests, and invite the people they want there. Leaders see every request in one place. Brothers can invite their own guests. Open the frat portal.

🔥 Every week

The Weekend Drop lands Thursdays at 6 PM. Save parties with ♡ for alerts, keep your streak going, and climb the Door Rush leaderboard for a free semester.

Safety first

Real names, real lists

Everyone signs up with a confirmed Penn State email, and hosts check names at the door. Outside guests are named in advance and vouched for by someone on the list.

Look out for each other

The Party Smart kit on the main page lets you share your location with a friend, set a check-in reminder, and report anything that's not right. If someone needs help, call 911. Penn State's Responsible Action Protocol is there so students call.

We act on reports

Reports go straight to the PhatFrats team. We remove parties and ban accounts that make nights unsafe.

Your privacy

  • We never sell your data, and we don't track you on other sites.
  • Passwords are stored only as scrambled hashes. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card.
  • PhatBot chats aren't saved. Outside guests' details are deleted two days after the party.
  • Email updates can be switched off any time in your profile.
