For sororities

When a fraternity invites your sorority to a party, every sister is on the list. No requests, no waiting.

  1. Log in
    Use your own PhatFrats account. Sign up with your Penn State email if you don't have one.
  2. Sign in to your sorority
    Sisters use the sister password from their chair. Chairs use their chair sign-in.
  3. Get the invites
    Frat invites to your sorority show up here and on your party map, with you already on the list.

Sorority passwords

The sister password lets sisters join. The chair password signs someone in as a chair, so only share it with people who run the sorority. Changing a password doesn't remove anyone, so also remove people who left from the Sisters tab.
