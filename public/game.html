    How to play

    • Send each guest to their party before they reach the red line: ★ star → Neon, ● circle → Chill, ◆ diamond → Costume.
    • Hooded guests with a red ✕ are crashers. Hit Bounce.
    • 3 mistakes and the doors close: a wrong door, a crasher getting in, a guest reaching the line, or bouncing someone who was invited.
    • From level 3 the doors switch places. Watch the labels and buttons.
    • Streaks raise your multiplier up to x5. 👑 VIPs are worth triple. Fill the Hype meter for 6 seconds of double points and slower guests.
    • Every 40 correct guests wins back a lost wristband, up to 3.
    • Keys: ← ↑ → to send, ↓ or Space to bounce, P to pause. On a phone, use the big buttons.
