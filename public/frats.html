For chapters

Put your parties on the PhatFrats map, answer invite requests from students and invite the people you want there.

  1. Log in
    Use your own PhatFrats account. Sign up with your Penn State email if you don't have one.
  2. Sign in to your chapter
    Brothers use the brother password from their leader. Chapter leaders use their leader sign-in.
  3. Post and invite
    Leaders post parties. Every brother can invite their own guests.

Your parties

Post a party

Invite someone

They're put straight on the list and get an email. If they're new to PhatFrats, they join the list as soon as they sign up with that email.

Bring someone from outside Penn State

One person per party, only at parties your leader opened to outside guests. You vouch for them, and they go straight on the list. They need a photo ID that matches the name.

Your invites

Everyone's invites

Every brother's personal invites. As leader you can take any of them back.

Invite requests

Students who asked to come. Full Access members are listed first. Approve puts them on the list. Waitlist tells them they're not in yet.

Outside guests

People who aren't Penn State students, each vouched for by someone on your list. Students' guests wait for you. Brothers' guests are already on the list. Approving emails the guest if they gave an email.
