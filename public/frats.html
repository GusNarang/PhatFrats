For chapters

Put your parties on the PhatFrats map, answer invite requests from students and invite the people you want there.

  1. Log in
    Use your own PhatFrats account. Sign up with your Penn State email if you don't have one.
  2. Sign in to your chapter
    Brothers use the brother password from their leader. Chapter leaders use their leader sign-in.
  3. Post and invite
    Leaders post parties. Every brother can invite their own guests.

Log in on the main site first, then come back to this page.

Brothers on PhatFrats

Chapter passwords

The brother password lets brothers join. The leader password signs someone in as a leader, so only share it with people who should run the chapter. Changing a password doesn't remove anyone, so also remove people who left from the Members tab.

Running the chapter? Enter the leader password to become a leader.
